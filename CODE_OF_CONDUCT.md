# Code of Conduct

LightL follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers, accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior: harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized content, and spam.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block contributors who violate this code.

Report violations privately through [GitHub's report-content tool](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or by contacting the maintainer [@Puhavik](https://github.com/Puhavik) via their GitHub profile. All reports are handled confidentially.
